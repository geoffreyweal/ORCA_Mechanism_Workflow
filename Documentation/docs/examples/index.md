# Examples

The repository's `examples/` folder holds complete projects: xyz files plus `project.yaml`. Copy one and
run `mechflow validate` in it to see how a project is put together.

| example | species | steps | mechanisms | what it shows |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [HCN → HNC](hcn.md) | 2 | 1 | 1 | the whole procedure in minutes; a test of your set-up |

The pages under *Setting up a reaction* explain the rest (acid catalysis, balancing, two products) with a
second small reaction: the acid-catalysed opening of propylene oxide by methanol. Its network map is on the
[Network maps](../maps.md#examples) page.
